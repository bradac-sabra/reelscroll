# ReelScroll

A browser extension that turns Reddit into a full-screen, vertical video feed —
scroll through the video posts of any subreddit one at a time, the way
short-video apps work.

Works on www.reddit.com and old.reddit.com, with Reddit-hosted videos,
crossposts, GIFs, Imgur, Streamable and YouTube posts.

## Privacy

ReelScroll collects no data. There are no servers, no analytics and no tracking —
it reads the same post listing the Reddit page already shows, directly from
Reddit.
