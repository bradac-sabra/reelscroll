# ReelScroll – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 September 2026_

## Summary

ReelScroll does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It has no
servers, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the extension does

When you turn on the video feed on a Reddit page, ReelScroll requests that
page's public post listing from Reddit — the same data the page itself shows —
and plays the video posts in a full-screen, vertical feed. These requests go
directly from your browser to Reddit, using your existing Reddit session,
exactly as if you browsed Reddit normally.

To play videos, the extension loads them directly from the hosts where they are
stored (for example v.redd.it, Imgur, Streamable, Redgifs or YouTube). For some
hosts it asks the host's public API for the video file address. Only the video's
public ID is sent.

## Data collection

- No personal information, browsing history, account data or usage statistics
  are collected.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party other than the video
  hosts listed above, which are contacted only to play the video you are
  viewing.
- The extension does not use cookies or local storage of its own.

## Permissions

- **Access to reddit.com** — to show the video feed on Reddit pages and read the
  post listing.
- **Access to v.redd.it, api.streamable.com and redgifs.com** — to look up and
  load video streams.
- **`scripting`** — to start the feed on a Reddit tab that was already open
  before the extension was installed.
- **`declarativeNetRequest`** — to omit the Referer header on video requests to
  one video host that otherwise refuses playback outside its own site. No other
  requests are modified.
